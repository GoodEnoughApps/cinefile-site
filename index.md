## Privacy Policy

CineFile is a personal tracker for movies and TV shows you've watched. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I don't sell data, and I don't collect your name, email or any account information.

### What stays on your device

Your library is stored locally on your device. That includes titles, watch history, ratings, lists and favorites. I don't have access to it, and it isn't uploaded to a server I run.

### Backups and sync

- **iCloud Drive backup (optional).** If you choose a backup folder, CineFile writes a copy of your library into that folder. It is stored in your iCloud Drive and governed by Apple's privacy terms. I can't see it.
- **Exports and sharing.** CSV exports and shared lists are created only when you ask. They go wherever you send them, using the iOS share sheet. A shared list is a link containing the list's titles. Anyone with the link can read it.
- **Photos.** If you tap Save Image on a Monthly Review card, CineFile saves it to your Photos library. It has add-only access and can't read your photos.

### Information sent over the internet

To show posters, descriptions, cast, ratings, air dates and streaming availability, CineFile looks up titles from third-party metadata services: TMDB, TheTVDB, OMDb and Watchmode. Requests are routed through a proxy server I operate. The proxy holds the API credentials and forwards the request.

What those requests contain:

- the text you type in search;
- the title IDs of items you view or add;
- language settings;
- your device's region, taken from your iOS settings, which is used for age ratings and release dates.

The requests do not include your name, email, library contents, watch history or ratings.

My proxy doesn't keep request logs, so I don't store your searches or IP address. The proxy runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests in order to deliver them and may keep its own short-term operational records; see [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). My proxy doesn't pass your IP address on to the metadata services below; they see only my proxy's requests.

These services have their own privacy policies:

- [TMDB privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy)
- [TheTVDB privacy policy](https://thetvdb.com/privacy-policy)
- [OMDb legal information](https://www.omdbapi.com/legal.htm)
- [Watchmode privacy policy](https://www.watchmode.com/privacy)

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, CineFile schedules reminders (for example, for new episodes) locally on your device. No push server is involved.

### Optional links to other services

If you enter a Seerr/Overseerr address in Settings, CineFile builds a link to that server and opens it in your browser. The app doesn't contact that server itself; the link opens in your browser and includes the title's ID. Streaming buttons open the corresponding streaming app or website.

### Children

CineFile doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

### Your choices

You can delete everything CineFile stores by deleting the app. Delete the backup file in your iCloud Drive folder to remove that copy too. Because I hold no account data, there's nothing for me to delete on my side.
